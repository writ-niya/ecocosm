ART SLOTS
=========
Put paintings here, then list them in the ART block near the top of the
script in index.html.

  title          1920 x 1080   behind the title screen
  logo             small       beside the site name in the top bar
  keeper          600 x 800    your portrait on The Keeper screen
  regions/*      1200 x 700    one landscape per region
  sigils/*         square      an emblem per region, transparent PNG
  portraits/*     600 x 800    optional, one per entry — used in the codex
                               roster, the feature panel and entry headers

Anything left empty keeps the drawn fallback, so the site always works.

Where to get art:
  · commission an illustrator (this is what the reference sites did)
  · itch.io/game-assets — searchable, much of it cheap or free
  · unsplash.com — free photographs, good for region landscapes
