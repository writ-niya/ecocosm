This is a digital garden rather than a blog. Nothing here is arranged by date, and very little of it is finished.

## How it works
Every note carries a growth stage — seedling, budding, evergreen — so you can tell what is a passing thought and what has been sat with. Notes link to each other directly, and the Constellation page draws those links as a map. Collections group them by territory rather than by chronology.

## Why it is built this way
A blog rewards finished opinions and buries them the moment they scroll. A garden lets an idea stay visible and keep changing. Since most of what I think about takes years rather than posts, this suited the work better.

## Built with
Hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single file, reading markdown from this repository. No framework, no tracking, no comments section. Type is set in Grenze Gotisch, Parisienne, Cormorant Garamond, and IBM Plex Mono.
