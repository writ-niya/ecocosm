I write. That is the first and most stubborn fact about me — essays, fiction, and a great deal that never leaves the notebook.

Most of what I make circles one question: who holds the authority to narrate the natural world, and what colonialism did to that authority. It shows up as political writing about conservation and the Global South, and as fiction — a novel drawing on non-Western mythology to work the same argument from the inside.

This site is the working room rather than the finished shelf. Most of it is unfinished by design; see the [[Territory Map]] for where the thinking is headed.
