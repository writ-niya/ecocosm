Who holds authority to narrate the natural world — and what colonialism did to that authority. Fiction, political writing, and worldbuilding are three registers of one question.

## People & concepts to hold
- Fortress conservation — parks built by excluding the people who lived there first
- Green colonialism — climate urgency reproducing old displacement
- Rob Nixon, *Slow Violence and the Environmentalism of the Poor*
- Amitav Ghosh, *The Nutmeg's Curse*
- Linda Tuhiwai Smith, *Decolonizing Methodologies*
- Land Back; Indigenous Protected and Conserved Areas

> the map is not the territory, but somebody drew the map
