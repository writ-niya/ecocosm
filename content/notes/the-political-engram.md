# The rules I edit by
- One controlling argument per piece. Everything else serves it or goes.
- Start somewhere specific and unexpected, then open out into the larger politics.
- Abstract ideas have to land in material reality — supply chains, goods, lived experience.
- Source from non-obvious places.
