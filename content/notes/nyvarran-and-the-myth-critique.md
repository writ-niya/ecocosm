Six arcana, six houses, six patron animals — Nyvarran runs on the idea that a philosophy of nature can also be a form of political power. Which is, unintentionally, the argument in [[Territory Map]] played out as fiction first.

Worth testing: what would it mean for a house's claim to land to carry the weight real relationships to land carry, rather than remaining pure aesthetic?
