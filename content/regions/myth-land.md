# Why Myth Counts as Evidence
^ the argument of this region
A story about a river that must not be crossed is doing several things at once. It is a
prohibition, a memory, and a claim about who belongs to that place. Read as literature it
is charming. Read as evidence it is a land record.

This region collects the ones I keep returning to.
:::

::: table | Term | What it means here
cosmology :: the shape a culture believes the world has
tenure :: who a place recognises as belonging to it

:::
